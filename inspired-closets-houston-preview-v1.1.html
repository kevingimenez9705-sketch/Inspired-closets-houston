Questions We Hear Often

Have questions?
We have answers.

What is a "custom closet," and why get one?
A custom closet is designed around your space, your belongings, and your habits — not a generic rod-and-shelf setup. Most closets are built with minimal thought, which leaves space under-used and clutter piling up; a custom system uses every inch to fit how you actually live.
How much does a custom closet cost?
It depends on the size of the space, how it's configured, materials, and how many custom features you add. Smaller closets typically start around $5,000 — the general rule is more product means more cost.
Can I see my design before I commit?
Yes. Every project includes a 3D rendering, and our Envision augmented-reality tool lets you walk through your new closet in its finished state before anything is built.
What sets Inspired Closets apart?
Free in-home consultations, one-on-one designer collaboration, a process built around listening first, USA-manufactured furniture-grade materials, and Envision AR so nothing is a surprise on install day.
